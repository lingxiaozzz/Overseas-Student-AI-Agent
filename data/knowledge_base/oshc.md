# Overseas Student Health Cover

Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is health insurance required for most international students on an Australian student visa.

Key points:

- OSHC should usually cover the full duration of the student visa.
- Students should keep their OSHC policy certificate because it may be needed for visa and university processes.
- OSHC can help cover doctor visits, hospital treatment, ambulance services, and some prescription medicines, depending on the policy.
- Students should check waiting periods, exclusions, claim process, and emergency contact numbers.
- OSHC is not the same as travel insurance. Students may still want travel insurance for luggage, flights, or non-medical travel risks.

Students should confirm the latest OSHC and visa requirements through official Australian government and insurer sources.
