# USYD Orientation Week

Orientation week helps new international students settle into the University of Sydney before classes begin.

## What happens during orientation

- Welcome sessions for new students, including international student briefings.
- Campus tours covering key buildings, libraries, student centres, and transport links.
- Information about enrolment confirmation, timetable access, and student ID collection.
- Introduction to academic support services, counselling, and international student advisers.
- Sessions on OSHC, banking, mobile phone setup, and local transport (Opal card).
- Social events and faculty-specific welcome activities to meet other students.

## What students should do

- Check the official USYD orientation schedule on the student portal before arrival.
- Bring passport, visa grant notice, CoE, and OSHC details for on-campus registration if required.
- Attend the international student session and faculty welcome for your program.
- Visit the library and learning hubs to understand study resources and academic integrity rules.
- Collect or activate your student ID and confirm enrolment status is active.
- Note emergency contacts, campus security numbers, and after-hours support options.

## Campus basics

- The main Camperdown/Darlington campus is close to Central Station and multiple bus routes.
- Students can use the official university website and campus maps to find lecture theatres, libraries, and student services.
- Orientation staff can help with directions, building names, and first-week class locations.

Students should confirm the latest orientation dates, session times, and mandatory activities through the official University of Sydney website and student portal.
