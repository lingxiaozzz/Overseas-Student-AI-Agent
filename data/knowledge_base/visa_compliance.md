# Student Visa Compliance and Work Rights

This guide summarises common compliance expectations for international students on an Australian student visa. It is not legal advice.

## Core visa obligations

- Hold a valid student visa and comply with visa conditions at all times.
- Maintain enrolment in a registered course and meet course attendance and progress requirements.
- Keep Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the required period.
- Notify the university and update visa records if contact details (including a residential address) or personal circumstances change.
- Do not rely on unverified "visa shortcuts", fake documents, or advice that bypasses official processes.

## Work rights on a student visa

- Student visa holders are generally allowed to work up to a limited number of hours while their course is in session, subject to current government rules.
- Unlimited work hours may apply during scheduled course breaks, depending on the visa conditions in force at the time.
- Students must check their visa grant notice and the official Department of Home Affairs guidance for the exact hour limits that apply to them.
- Paid work must follow Australian workplace laws, including minimum wage and tax obligations.
- Students should not accept "cash-only illegal work", undeclared employment, or jobs that require breaching visa conditions.

## What to refuse or avoid

- Do not follow instructions to ignore university, immigration, or health insurance rules.
- Do not use fake visa approvals, forged documents, or third-party "guaranteed visa" schemes.
- Do not pursue illegal work arrangements offered as easy income options.
- Do not share passport, visa, or banking details with unverified agents promising shortcuts.

## If a student is unsure

- Check the official Australian Government Department of Home Affairs website for visa conditions and work rights.
- Contact the University of Sydney international student support team for enrolment and compliance questions.
- Seek help from a registered migration agent or qualified legal adviser for complex visa matters.

Students must verify all visa, work, and compliance requirements through official Australian government and university sources.
