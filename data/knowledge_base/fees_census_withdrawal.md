# USYD Fees, Census Dates, and Withdrawal Basics

This guide gives general decision support for international students. Fees, dates, refund rules, and academic consequences can change, so students must confirm the current details in the University of Sydney student portal and official policy pages.

## Before census date

- Check each unit's census date as soon as you enrol; different teaching periods can have different dates.
- Review tuition fees, payment due dates, and any scholarship or sponsorship conditions linked to your enrolment.
- If a unit does not fit your plan, seek course advice early rather than waiting until the deadline.
- Keep screenshots or written confirmations of enrolment changes and payment records.

## If you are considering withdrawal

- First identify whether you want to change one unit, reduce your study load, defer, or discontinue your course; these actions can have different academic and visa consequences.
- Check the relevant deadline before submitting a withdrawal request. A late change can affect fee liability, academic record outcomes, or both.
- International students should consider whether changing load affects their Confirmation of Enrolment, progression plan, scholarship, accommodation, or visa conditions.
- Ask the appropriate university team for written advice when exceptional circumstances, illness, or a late withdrawal are involved.

## Safe next steps

1. Confirm the unit, teaching period, and deadline in the student portal.
2. Read the current official withdrawal and fee information.
3. Contact course planning or international student support if the change may affect your study load or visa.
4. Keep evidence of the request and the final outcome.

Do not rely on old social-media advice or another student's deadline; verify the current policy for your own enrolment.
