# Sydney Student Accommodation Basics

International students in Sydney commonly choose between university accommodation, private student accommodation, shared apartments, homestay, or short-term temporary housing.

Practical housing advice:

- Avoid paying deposits before verifying the property, contract, and landlord or provider.
- Inspect the room in person or through a trusted video inspection where possible.
- Check commute time to campus, public transport access, weekly rent, bills, bond, and minimum lease period.
- Common suburbs for University of Sydney students include Camperdown, Darlington, Glebe, Newtown, Redfern, Chippendale, and Ultimo.
- Budget for bond, rent in advance, utilities, internet, furniture, kitchen items, and transport.
- Keep written records of payments and agreements.

Students who are unsure should start with short-term accommodation and search for long-term housing after arrival.
