# Academic Support and Integrity at USYD

Academic success involves both using support early and following integrity requirements. Specific procedures and penalties can change, so students should check current University of Sydney guidance for their unit and faculty.

## Academic support

- Read each unit outline early for assessment format, marking criteria, attendance expectations, and submission rules.
- Use teaching staff, tutors, library services, learning hubs, and approved academic-skills support when you need help with writing, study planning, or understanding an assessment.
- Start assignments early enough to ask questions before the deadline.
- Keep drafts, notes, research records, and feedback so you can show how your work was developed.

## Academic integrity principles

- Submit work that is genuinely your own unless collaboration is explicitly allowed.
- Cite sources using the citation style required by the unit.
- Do not copy from another student, buy assignments, submit recycled work without permission, or use unapproved assistance.
- Treat AI tools carefully: follow the unit's current rules, disclose use where required, and do not present generated text or analysis as your own work when that is not permitted.

## If you are unsure

1. Read the unit outline and assessment instructions.
2. Ask the coordinator or tutor what collaboration and tool use are allowed.
3. Use university academic-support services rather than unverified online providers.
4. Keep a record of the advice you receive.

When in doubt, ask before submitting; a short clarification is safer than an integrity breach.
