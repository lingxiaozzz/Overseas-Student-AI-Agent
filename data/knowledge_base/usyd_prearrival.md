# USYD Pre-arrival Checklist

This guide is for international students preparing to study at the University of Sydney.

Before arriving in Sydney, students should:

- Accept their offer and check the Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE).
- Apply for the correct Australian student visa.
- Arrange Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the full visa period.
- Book temporary accommodation before arrival if long-term housing is not confirmed.
- Prepare digital and printed copies of passport, visa grant notice, CoE, OSHC policy, academic documents, and emergency contacts.
- Check the University of Sydney student portal for enrolment, timetable, and orientation information.
- Bring a payment card that works internationally and enough funds for the first few weeks.
- Plan transport from Sydney Airport to the first accommodation.

Students should always confirm visa, enrolment, and compliance requirements with official university and Australian government sources.
