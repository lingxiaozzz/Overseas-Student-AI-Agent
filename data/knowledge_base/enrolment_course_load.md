# USYD Enrolment and Course Load Basics

This guide covers stable planning principles for international students. Unit availability, deadlines, progression requirements, and visa conditions must be checked against current official University of Sydney and Australian Government information.

## Enrolment checks

- Confirm that your enrolment is active and that your selected units fit your degree structure.
- Check prerequisite, corequisite, and credit-point requirements before changing units.
- Review your timetable for clashes, campus location, and assessment timing.
- Keep your contact details current in the student system and read official messages about enrolment actions.

## Study load decisions

- A reduced load, leave of absence, deferral, or course change can have different academic, financial, scholarship, and visa effects.
- Discuss a proposed change with academic or course-planning staff before assuming that it is permitted.
- International students should seek written guidance when a change could affect course progression or student-visa compliance.
- Do not drop a unit simply because it is difficult without first checking deadlines and support options.

## Practical workflow

1. Review your degree plan and current unit requirements.
2. Check the official deadline and the impact of the intended change.
3. Ask for academic advice if prerequisites, progression, or graduation timing are unclear.
4. Ask international student support about any possible visa impact.
5. Save confirmation of the final enrolment change.

For current requirements, use the official student portal and your faculty's course-planning guidance.
